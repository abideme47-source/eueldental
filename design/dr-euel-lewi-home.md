# Dr Euel Lewi Dental Homepage

Source: Figma file `jk5bceehzw5fJab2LC0IY7`, frame `1:2` (`Dental_Design`).

## Visual system

- Desktop source frame: 1920px wide, 8474px tall; centered content is approximately 1240px wide.
- Fonts: Manrope for headings, navigation, and buttons; Archivo for body copy; Baskervville for the clinic wordmark.
- Colors: brand blue `#3267FF`, CTA blue `#1644EF`, navy `#000A2D`, pale blue `#F1F8FF`, body gray `#636571`, white `#FFFFFF`.
- Corner radius is primarily 4px. Layout uses spacious white sections, restrained shadows, and photographic panels.

## Page order

1. Contact strip and navigation with the clinic wordmark and appointment action.
2. Pale-blue hero with “Helping You to Bring Back Your Happy Smile,” supporting copy, dentist photo, floating 1,200-customer panel, and appointment booking bar.
3. Three centered benefits: Comfortable Visits, Experienced Dentists, and Satisfactory Service.
4. About section with dentist collage, clinic introduction, four check items, and service links.
5. Care confidence section with progress bars, doctor image, and consultation hours.
6. Pale-blue statistics strip: 1,200+ happy clients, 8+ years, 200+ online appointments, all ages welcome.
7. Clinic technology and all-ages photo mosaic.
8. Six service cards under “What’s Bothering Your Smile?”: Tooth Decay, Yellow Teeth, Crooked Teeth, Tooth Pain, Broken Tooth, and Missing Teeth.
9. Patient testimonials.
10. “Let Us Take Care of Your Smile” image banner.
11. Three dental advice cards under “Quick Answers to Common Questions.”
12. Contact call-to-action and dark four-column footer.
